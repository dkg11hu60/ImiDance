4. Your Rights Regarding Your Personal Data

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights, which you can exercise free of charge:

  • Right of Online Access, Correction, and Deletion: You do not need manual intervention from the Data Controller. By logging into the registration application, you can **correct (modify) your data in real-time, or permanently delete your entire user profile and history with a single click** under the Profile menu.
  • Right to Restriction of Processing: You can request the restriction of processing of your personal data if you dispute its accuracy or object to the processing.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You are entitled to withdraw your consent at any time. The withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.
  • Right to Data Portability: You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format, or have it transferred directly to another controller.
