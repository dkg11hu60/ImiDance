Course

Standard & Latin Movement Technique Development Course

Do you feel like you already know the figures, but your movement is not yet quite what you want it to be? It's time to return to the basics. The best dancers are distinguished not by the number of figures they know, but by the quality of their technique — a well-constructed movement provides more stable balance, more efficient power transfer, and a more confident performance.

Course Objectives

  • Conscious development of Standard and Latin-American techniques.
  • Correct body placement, weight transfer, footwork, and spin techniques.
  • Practical application of technical elements through figures.

Syllabus

  • Standard and Latin walk and step techniques.
  • Spin technique fundamentals and refinement.
  • Integration of learned elements into figures and choreographies.

What to Expect

  • An easy-to-understand, logical teaching system.
  • Detailed, crystal-clear technical explanations.
  • Immediately applicable exercises with many useful details.
  • Continuous correction and personalized feedback.

About Me

Imre Balázs — Dance Teacher

I started over 30 years ago as a competitive dancer. After ten successful years of competing, I earned my dance teacher degree at the Hungarian Dance Academy. Since then, I have been training competitors, art school students, and adult/senior couples — both in Hungary and the United Kingdom.

My primary profile is the 10 dance disciplines of ballroom dancing: Standard — English Waltz, Tango, Quickstep, Slow Foxtrot, Viennese Waltz; Latin-American — Samba, Cha-Cha-Cha, Rumba, Paso Doble, Jive. Throughout my career, I have always emphasized correct technical foundations and requirements, and consistent hard work has brought its rewards year after year.

  • 2003My competitive couple (Szabolcs Marton & Petra Darók) won 2nd place in the Hungarian Championship in JUN-II Latin; they were delegated by the Hungarian DanceSport Association to the WDSF JUN-II Latin World Championship in Riga.
  • 2002–08Awarded "Coach of Student Olympiad Champions" in Szolnok for six consecutive years; competitors of Pelikán TSE achieved consistent podium finishes.
  • 2004Zánka Student Olympiad — 6 gold medals; Pelikán TSE placed 2nd nationwide out of more than 100 clubs.
  • 2011Both of my girls' formation groups (Smile, ZÚZ-Dance) achieved gold qualification at the Performing Arts Schools Dance Festival in Túrkeve.
  • 2014–24Based in England, I held courses, private lessons, and led Standard and Latin workshops for adult and senior couples.

“Without technique, your dance has no quality; without quality, your dance is valueless.”

— Edita Daniūtė – Mirko Gozzoli

Technique is the "grammar" of dance. Many dancers fall into the trap of believing that the more steps they know, the better they dance — but a crowd of steps leads astray. Dance fewer figures, but with proper technique and style, and put quality into your dance. Wherever you go, everyone will notice the difference.
